# Manuel Ferreras

**`Co-Founder and CEO of Ringo`**

We put prediction markets inside the conversation: a bot turns arguments on X and Kick into funded markets, traded in USDC without leaving the feed. Live on mainnet since September 2025, settling on-chain, with protocol fees collected every calendar month since launch. Latin American football is our first wedge.

I am a technical founder. Before Ringo I spent four years building wallet and payments infrastructure, including account abstraction and gas sponsorship, which are the same rails Ringo runs on today. I wrote most of our settlement chain myself, so when I describe the protocol, I am describing something I built.
